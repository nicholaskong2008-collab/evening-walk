Most evenings my dad and I go for a walk, and most evenings the conversation ends up on the economy. A headline about interest rates turns into an argument about mortgages. A story about tariffs turns into a debate about who really pays them.

This blog is where I take those conversations further. Each essay starts with a real event, then works back to the economics underneath it: the incentives, the trade-offs, and the numbers that decide who wins and who loses.

## Who I am

I'm Nicholas Kong, a student taking A-levels in Economics, Maths, Further Maths and Physics. I'm interested in how policy decisions play out in the real world, and I find the maths behind them easier to trust when I can see the data.

## How I write these

- I start from a reliable source, usually a major newspaper or official data, and link to it at the end of every essay.
- I try to explain one economic idea well rather than several badly.
- When I use a number, I say where it came from.

If you spot a mistake or disagree with an argument, I'd like to hear it.

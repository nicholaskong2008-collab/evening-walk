Most evenings my dad and I go for a walk, and the conversation almost always turns to the news. Usually it's something economic: an interest-rate decision, a trade dispute, a company's results.

We rarely agree, and we rarely have the numbers to hand. This blog is my way of going back to those conversations with the data, working out what economics actually says, and writing it down clearly enough that someone who wasn't on the walk can follow it.

Each essay will start with a real event and work back to the idea underneath it.
